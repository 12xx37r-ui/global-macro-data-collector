V2.9 changes:
1) Adds column-profile inference: every plausible date/value column pair is scored by valid monthly row overlap.
2) Supports shifted data columns after metadata rows instead of assuming the header columns contain the series.
3) Extends date parsing for numeric YYYYMM/YYYMMDD, numeric-as-text Excel serials, day-first month-name dates, and common separators.
4) Normalizes Unicode minus/dashes and simple footnote markers in numeric cells.
5) Keeps the existing vertical + transposed/wide parser and chooses the richest valid series.
6) No extra network source or retry was added. GSCPI remains one New York Fed workbook request, then local last-good only.
